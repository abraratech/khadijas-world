Khadija's World Accessibility Statement

Version 1.0 — reviewed 1 August 2026

The project aims to make core setup, menus, dialogue, safety controls, and recovery paths usable across keyboard, touch, and common assistive settings.

Available features

  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus for interface controls.
  • Arrow keys or W, A, S, and D for movement.
  • Screen-reader status announcements for important actions.
  • Gentle motion, larger words, stronger colors, and instant dialogue settings.
  • System reduced-motion preference respected during first launch.
  • Mobile safe areas, software-keyboard viewport handling, and 44-pixel minimum touch controls.
  • Focus trapping and restoration for dialogs and popovers.

Known limitation

The 3D play area does not yet provide a complete non-visual equivalent for every spatial object and free-form activity. Menus, settings, chat, privacy controls, save controls, and error recovery are the strongest accessible paths.

Support

Accessibility problems can be reported through Khadija's World project support. Include the browser, device, input method, and the step that was difficult.
