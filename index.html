Plain-Language Privacy Notice

Khadija's World does not require an account. It does not use advertising, analytics, external AI, or remote NPC chat.

World progress and bounded conversation memories stay in this browser. Players can turn memories off, clear them, export a save, or import a trusted save file.

Clearing browser storage may remove progress. Please do not enter real names, addresses, school details, passwords, or other personal information in NPC chat.

Version 1.0 — effective 24 July 2026. This is a product privacy notice, not a claim of guaranteed legal compliance.

Open-Source Notices

Babylon.js and TypeScript are provided under Apache-2.0 licenses. Vite and Vitest are provided under MIT licenses.

The game’s procedural geometry, authored dialogue, interface, and synthesized sound are original project work. No third-party model, texture, font, music, or sound file is distributed.
