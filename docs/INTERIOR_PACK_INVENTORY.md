## Import rules

1. Convert only approved objects to GLB; do not load the entire pack.
2. Imported meshes are visual shells, never gameplay geometry.
3. Existing invisible proxies retain collisions, seats, storage, placement,
   interaction ownership, and save IDs.
4. Recolor and re-light imported pieces to match the game's toy-sheen finish.
5. High may use the imported shell; Low keeps the current procedural object.
6. Reject any object that creates a visible style mismatch.

## ART.1H status

ART.1H keeps all 123 Blend files outside the browser runtime. Eight bedroom
candidates are catalogued in `src/game/assets/interiorPackCatalog.ts`, and
`scripts/export-interior-bedroom.ps1` can export local review GLBs through an
installed Blender copy. The approved ART.1H bedroom remains project-authored
procedural geometry until an individual imported object demonstrably improves
style without harming performance or interaction alignment.

## ART.1I status

ART.1I also keeps the full Blend archive outside the browser runtime. Twelve
café and grocery candidates are catalogued in
`src/game/assets/interiorPackCatalog.ts`, and
`scripts/export-interior-commercial.ps1` can export local review GLBs through
an installed Blender copy. The approved ART.1I café and grocery layers remain
project-authored procedural geometry until an individual imported object proves
visibly better and passes performance, licence, and interaction-alignment review.

## ART.1K-A status

Twenty exported GLBs passed geometry and integrity review. Fourteen unique models
are active in 22 High-only placements; six remain review-only. Runtime assets
live under `public/assets/interiors/ultimate-house-v1/` and are loaded only when
their location is first visited. See `docs/ART1KA_SELECTIVE_INTERIORS.md` for the
accept/reject matrix and `art/ASSET_MANIFEST.json` for hashes and mesh counts.
