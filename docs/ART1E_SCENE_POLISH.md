# ART.1E Scene Polish

ART.1E adds a non-interactive visual layer to all six locations. The existing
geometry remains authoritative for movement, seats, storage, recipes, doors,
hotspots, and save compatibility.

## Family home

- baseboards and layered wall art
- patterned living-room rug
- warm pendant lights
- kitchen backsplash tiles and cabinet pulls
- additional plant silhouette

## Bedroom

- baseboards and patterned rug
- wall art and string lights
- tufted headboard details
- layered soft pillows
- additional plant

## Neighborhood

- storefront cornices
- flower boxes and flowers
- pavement joints and road-edge strips
- stylized street lamps
- additional planter

## Sunny Café

- baseboards and patterned rug
- pendant lights and framed wall art
- tiled counter front
- table vases and flowers
- pastry silhouettes inside the display

## Park

- background tree clusters
- pond border stones
- stepping-stone path
- picnic bunting
- additional flowers

## Grocery

- aisle headers
- stocked shelf silhouettes
- produce crates and fruit clusters
- checkout belt and receipt roll
- fridge handles and pendant lights

Every added mesh is non-pickable and tagged as a decorative detail. Low quality
can disable the complete pass without affecting gameplay.
